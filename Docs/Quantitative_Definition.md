 # Site risks 

| Indicator                     | Low risk  | Medium risk | High risk |
| ----------------------------- | --------- | ----------- | --------- |
| Recruitment performance ratio | ≥ 1.00    | 0.70 - 0.99 | < 0.70    |
| Operational reliability       | ≥ 85%     | 70 - 84%    | < 70%     |

# Overall risk classification
The overall site risk level is determined by combining three dimensions: activation delay, recruitment performance and operational reliability. Each dimension is classified independently as Low, Medium or High risk. The overall classification is then assigned according to the number and combination of risk categories.

| Low               | Medium               | High                   |
| ----------------- | -------------------- | -----------------------|
| 3 Lows            | 1 High + 2 Low       | 2 High + any n° Medium |
| 2 Lows + 1 Medium | 1 High + 1 Med + Low | 1 High + 2 Medium      |
|                   | 1 Low  + 2 Medium    |                        |
